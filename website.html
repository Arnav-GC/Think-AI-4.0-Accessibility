Simple 3-Step Setup for Any User:

  1. Download & Extract: Download the omniaccess-ai-extension.zip file above and extract / unzip it to any folder on your computer.
  2. Open Chrome Extensions: In Chrome (or Edge/Brave), navigate to chrome://extensions and switch on the Developer mode toggle in the top-right corner.
  3. Load Extension: Click the "Load unpacked" button in the top-left corner and select the extracted folder. OmniAccess AI is now installed and ready to assist you on any website!
